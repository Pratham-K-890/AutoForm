Chrome Extension
Your interface for filling Google Forms with AI
Download Extension
1
Download & install — click Download above, unzip, then open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and click Load unpacked.
2
Open a Google Form — navigate to any Google Form in Chrome. The AutoForm icon in the toolbar will light up.
3
Click Analyze — the extension reads all questions and sends them to a council of 3 AI models (LLaMA 3.3 70B, Mixtral 8×7B, Gemini 2.0 Flash) which vote on every answer.
4
Review & Fill — check the AI answers, fill in any personal info fields, then click Fill. The extension injects answers directly into the form.
