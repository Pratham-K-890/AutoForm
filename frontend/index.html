How it works
Three steps to a filled form
No config, no API keys on your end. Install the extension and go.

Open a Google Form

Navigate to any Google Form in Chrome. The extension automatically detects it and shows a ready indicator in your toolbar.

Click Analyze

The extension reads every question from the page DOM and sends them to a council of three AI models — LLaMA, Mixtral, and Gemini — which vote on each answer.

Review & Fill

See every AI answer with confidence scores. Add your personal info for sensitive fields, then click Fill — answers are injected directly into the form.

Features
Everything you need
🗳️

3-Model Voting Council

LLaMA 3.3 70B, Mixtral 8×7B, and Gemini 2.0 Flash each answer independently. Majority vote wins — tiebreak goes to LLaMA.

🔒

Personal Info Protection

Name, email, phone, and address questions are flagged automatically. The AI never guesses — you fill those in yourself.

⚡

Works in Your Browser

The extension fills the form using the real DOM — no screenshot tricks, no Playwright, no external browser launched.

📋

All Question Types

Short text, paragraph, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdowns, date, and time — every native Google Forms type is supported.

🎯

Confidence Scores

Each answer shows how many models agreed. Full agreement is shown in green; split votes are flagged so you can review.

🌐

Deployed & Open Source

Self-host it or use the live backend at autoform-7lmx.onrender.com. Full source available on GitHub.
